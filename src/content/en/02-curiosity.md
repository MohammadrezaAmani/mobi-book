# The power of curiosity

Curiosity is the art of seeing familiar things as though they were new.

## Better questions

Instead of asking why an approach failed, ask what assumptions made it appear reasonable in the first place.

> The quality of our answers rarely exceeds the quality of our questions.

## A practical exercise

1. Choose an everyday phenomenon.
2. Write five questions about it.
3. Propose two explanations for each question.
4. Ask what evidence would prove you wrong.

| Approach    | Main question  | Outcome     |
| ----------- | -------------- | ----------- |
| Observation | What happened? | Description |
| Explanation | Why?           | Hypothesis  |
| Experiment  | How to test?   | Evidence    |
