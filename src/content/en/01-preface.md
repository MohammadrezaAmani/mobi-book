# A note before we begin

> The beginning of wisdom is curiosity.

This is a book about **thinking with care**. We will explore how questions become hypotheses, how hypotheses become arguments, and how ideas turn into things worth sharing.

## An invitation

Great ideas do not require complicated language. They require attention, imagination, and the willingness to change one's mind.

- Ask better questions.
- Look for evidence.
- Write down what you learn.
- Stay curious.

This example book is written in Markdown and published as a static React website with Mobi.
